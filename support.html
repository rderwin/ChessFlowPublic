Frequently asked

Does ChessFlow work offline?
Yes. Lessons, openings, puzzles, the coach, and the chess engine all run entirely on your device — no internet connection needed.
What does the full unlock include?
A one‑time in‑app purchase unlocks the complete coaching experience. Any of the support tiers unlocks the same full app — they're simply "pay what feels right" levels.
I bought the unlock but it's not showing — or I got a new device.
Open the unlock screen and tap Restore Purchases. That re‑checks your Apple ID and re‑applies anything you've already bought, at no extra charge. Make sure you're signed in with the same Apple ID used for the original purchase.
How is my data handled?
Everything stays on your device. ChessFlow has no accounts, no tracking, and no analytics. See our Privacy Policy for details.
How do I reset my progress?
You can clear stats and progress from the app's Settings. Deleting the app also removes all of its data from your device.
I found a bug or want to suggest a feature.
Please email akiratoriniku@gmail.com with as much detail as you can (what you did, what happened, your device and iOS version). It genuinely helps.
